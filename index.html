The problem

Customer purchase orders arrive in many formats and require repetitive interpretation, data extraction, SKU checks, and preparation before SAP entry.

The solution

ORDERPATH combines OCR, AI extraction, validation logic, SKU matching, customer lookup, and Excel automation into a human-in-the-loop workflow.

THE JOURNEY

From training experiments to operational systems.

The portfolio did not begin with ORDERPATH. It grew out of years of experimenting with better ways to train people, simulate real work, and use AI to solve specific problems.

2+ YEARS AGOCALLPATH begins

The AI journey starts with text-based customer call simulations, long before voice support existed. The goal was already the same: realistic practice built around the actual job.

Structured training modules are built for Microsoft Teams, creating guided, self-paced learning experiences that complement instructor-led training.

The same workflow-first thinking moves from training into production automation. ORDERPATH launches in Columbus and quickly begins expanding across branches.

2026 + NEXTThe portfolio accelerates

ORDERPATH scales nationally while QUOTEPATH, PURCHASEPATH, PRESSPATH, STOCKPATH and other systems extend the approach into new operational problems.

THE APPROACH

Built for people, not AI demos.

The goal is not automation for its own sake. The goal is to remove repetitive friction, make complicated work easier to execute, preserve human judgment where it matters, and create capacity that teams can use on higher-value work.

01Start with the workflow

Understand what people actually do before trying to automate it.

02Keep humans in the loop

Use AI to assist interpretation while keeping review and judgment where they belong.

03Measure what matters

Track usage, capacity, quality, and operational impact instead of novelty.
