Customer purchase orders arrive in many formats and require repetitive interpretation, data extraction, SKU checks, and preparation before SAP entry.

The solution

THE APPROACH

Built for people, not AI demos.

The goal is not automation for its own sake. The goal is to remove repetitive friction, make complicated work easier to execute, preserve human judgment where it matters, and create capacity that teams can use on higher-value work.

01Start with the workflow

Understand what people actually do before trying to automate it.

02Keep humans in the loop

Use AI to assist interpretation while keeping review and judgment where they belong.

03Measure what matters

Track usage, capacity, quality, and operational impact instead of novelty.
